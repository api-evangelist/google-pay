Marking the first anniversary of the Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol, this post explores how the communication standard lets AI agents collaborate securely and autonomously while preserving data protection and context. It showcases FoldRun for protein structure prediction and emerging A2A use cases across commerce, enterprise data, DevOps, and regulated sectors.
