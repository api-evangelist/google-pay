Google launches the TPU Developer Hub, an educational resource with code-first recipes, deep-dive documentation, and guidance to help developers unlock the full potential of Google Cloud TPUs. It spans hardware architecture, the software stack, debugging, optimization, and networking across the ML lifecycle from pre-training through inference.
